Managed IT you stop noticing

We take over full management of endpoints, servers and network. Monitoring, patching, licences and a service desk that picks up, for a fixed price per endpoint.

Book a meeting Contact

Who it is for

  • SMEs and organisations of 10 to 500 staff without an in-house IT team
  • Companies that find their current IT partner too slow or unclear
  • IT leads who want to outsource the work but keep control

What management covers

MON

Monitoring 24×7

Alerts on disks, backups, certificates and network, before your users notice.

PAT

Patch management

Windows, macOS and third-party updates in a tested window, reported monthly.

DSK

Service desk

One number, one mailbox. Dutch, English and French, remote and on-site.

LIC

Licences & assets

Current view of hardware, licences and warranties. No audit surprises.

What's included

  • Inventory of all devices, licences and warranties
  • Automatic updates for Windows, macOS and software
  • Monitoring of backups, disks and certificates
  • Service desk by phone, email and ticket portal
  • On- and offboarding of employees
  • Network, Wi-Fi and firewall management
  • Monthly report in plain language
  • Quarterly review with roadmap and budget

One point of contact

Predictable costs
