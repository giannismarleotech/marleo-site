MARLEO / DELIVERY

A clear route into everyday use.

01

Discover

Map the current process with its owners. Establish the source data, baseline, bottlenecks and a measurable acceptance test.

02

Design

Agree a bounded first release: users, integrations, access, budget, operating costs and responsibilities.

03

Build & validate

Test representative cases, missing information and failure scenarios with the people who will use the system.

04

Roll out & support

Agree migration, training, documentation, monitoring and support. Expand only after reviewing the first results.

MARLEO / CONTROL

Your team stays in control

Define who can view, edit and approve information. Agree retention, processing locations and provider dependencies before implementation. Sensitive actions require the review rules defined for your project.

Built to an agreed project scope. Existing APIs, data access and software licences are assessed before we confirm an integration.
