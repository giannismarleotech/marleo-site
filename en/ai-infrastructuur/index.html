Frequently asked

Do we need to replace our existing software?

Not necessarily. We assess available APIs and permissions across your CRM, email, documents and other tools.

Can the system act autonomously?

We agree this per task. Sending quotes or changing records can require human approval, with clear exception handling.

Where is our data processed?

Data flows, hosting, providers and access are agreed beforehand. Private or local deployment is an option where appropriate.

Who owns the code and system?

Ownership, code, documentation and handover are defined in the proposal. Third-party software and model licences still apply.

Do you still build websites and manage IT?

Yes. Websites, online stores, branding, development, games, managed IT, cloud and security remain available.
