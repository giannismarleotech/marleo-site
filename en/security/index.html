Who it is for

  • Companies covered by NIS2 or supplying to companies that are
  • Organisations that have already faced phishing or a hacked mailbox
  • Management teams that want to know how secure they really are

The baseline

MFA

Identity & MFA

Strong authentication, conditional access and privilege management.

EDR

Endpoint detection

EDR on every device, followed up by our service desk.

MAI

Email security

SPF, DKIM, DMARC enforcement and phishing filtering.

AWR

Awareness

Phishing simulations and short quarterly trainings.

What's included

  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) on every account
  • Endpoint detection on every laptop and server
  • Email security: SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up correctly
  • Backups following the 3-2-1 rule, with restore tests
  • Phishing simulation and short awareness training
  • Overview of who has access to what
  • Incident plan: who does what when things go wrong
  • Documentation for NIS2 and your insurer
