Questions before an enterprise project.

View website work
Do you work with larger European organisations?

Our project approach can be scoped for a department, a growing company or a larger organisation. We agree the required stakeholders, procurement steps, integrations, security requirements and delivery responsibilities during discovery.

Can you connect our existing CRM or ERP?

We assess available APIs, permissions, data quality and vendor constraints first. The implementation can connect existing software, extend a workflow or introduce a custom CRM where that makes sense.

Can we choose where our data is processed?

Hosting locations, AI providers, access and retention are project decisions. We assess the requested European hosting or private deployment requirements before proposing an architecture. We define the required controls and responsibilities in the project scope.

Do you still build websites?

Yes. Websites, online stores and branding remain available. Website projects are collected in Work, separately from the AI, CRM and IT focus of this homepage.

Which process could work better?

We start with your workflow, tools and goals, then agree a practical first step.
